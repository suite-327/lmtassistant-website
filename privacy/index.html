Privacy Policy

Last updated October 27, 2026

How LMT Assistant handles your information, and the information you keep about your clients.

LMT Assistant is made by Suite 327 Development LLC ("we", "us"). This policy covers the LMT Assistant app and the online booking pages it publishes at book.lmtassistant.com. It applies to two groups of people: therapists who use the app, and the clients whose details therapists keep in it or who use a therapist's booking page.

About HIPAA. We do not currently sign Business Associate Agreements (BAAs). If your practice is a HIPAA covered entity, for example because you bill health insurance electronically, LMT Assistant is not yet the right place to keep protected health information.

What we collect

From therapists

  • Account details: your name, email address and password, or your Apple or Google sign-in.
  • Business details: business name, services, prices, hours, locations, payment handles and booking-page settings.
  • Records you keep: clients, appointments, intake and consent forms, SOAP notes, invoices, income, expenses, mileage, credentials, professional contacts, and the photos and documents you upload.
  • Subscription status: whether you have an active subscription, from Apple through RevenueCat. We never see your payment card.
  • Technical data: a push notification token for your device, and crash reports.

About clients

  • What a therapist enters: for example a client's name, contact details, address, intake answers, consent records and session notes.
  • What a client sends through a booking page: their name, phone number, email address, a visit address for mobile sessions, notes for the therapist, signed consent, and messages sent with the Contact form. This goes to the therapist whose page it is.

We do not use advertising or analytics tracking in the app, and we do not sell anyone's information.

How we use it

  • To run the app: store and sync your records, show your booking page, and deliver requests from it to you.
  • To send notifications you have turned on, such as booking alerts and credential renewal reminders.
  • To read documents you choose to scan, as described below.
  • To manage your subscription, answer support requests, and keep the service secure, including investigating misuse.

We do not use client information for any purpose of our own.

How client details are protected

  • Client names, contact details, addresses, intake notes, SOAP notes and booking-page messages are encrypted with AES-256 before they are stored.
  • Everything travels between the app, booking pages and our servers over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.
  • Each account can reach only its own records. This is enforced in the database itself, not only in the app.
  • The app locks after 15 minutes without use, can require Face ID or a fingerprint to open, and supports a second sign-in step with an authenticator app.
  • We keep a log of sign-ins and of when client records are viewed, changed or exported. Entries cannot be edited or deleted.
  • Signed SOAP notes and signed client forms cannot be changed afterwards. Corrections are added as dated amendments.

To be plain about one point: this encryption keeps readable client details out of our database tables, but it is not end-to-end encryption. Our systems can derive the keys, which is how, for example, a booking request can be saved into your account while you are offline.

Scanning, dictation and AI

Where your phone supports it, the app reads documents on the phone itself. This includes booking screenshots, scanned intake forms and voice dictation, which never leave your device for this purpose.

If the phone cannot read a receipt, tax form, credential or insurance document, the app asks you first. Only with your permission does it send that one image to OpenAI to read. The request is made with storage turned off. Under OpenAI's API terms it is not used to train their models, and OpenAI may keep it for up to 30 days to monitor for abuse. You can always type the details in instead.

The Therapist Assistant on the Home screen files documents and runs commands on the phone itself where it can. Questions about massage practice, and requests to write a document, go to our AWS account to be answered. Names, phone numbers, emails and street addresses are removed from the question before it is sent.

The safety (SOS) button's emergency contact is stored only in your phone's secure Keychain. It is never sent to our servers. The location shown in an SOS text is added by your phone and goes through your own Messages app.

AI results can be wrong. Check what the app fills in before you save it.

How long we keep it

  • Your records are kept for as long as your account exists. Cancelling your subscription does not delete them.
  • Deleting your account (Settings) permanently deletes your records and uploaded files. Copies can remain in our hosting provider's backups until those backups expire on their regular schedule.
  • The security log of sign-ins and record access is kept for up to seven years, including after an account is deleted. It holds no client names or session notes.

Your choices and rights

  • See and export: client charts as PDFs, client lists and financial reports as spreadsheets, and full backups, all from inside the app.
  • Correct: edit your records in the app at any time.
  • Delete: delete individual records, or your whole account from Settings.
  • Limit: turn notifications off, and decline sending any document to OpenAI.

Depending on where you live, including Washington, Nevada and California, you may have further rights over your personal or health-related information. Email admin@suite-327.com to use them. We will not treat you differently for doing so.

If you are a therapist's client

Your therapist decides what is recorded about you and controls those records. To see, correct or delete them, contact your therapist. If you cannot reach them, email us and we will pass your request on and help them respond.

Children

The app is for licensed professionals and is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children through the app. A therapist may keep records about clients who are minors, with the consent their practice requires.

Security incidents

If we learn of a breach that affects your information, we will tell you, and anyone else we are required to, as the law requires.

Changes to this policy

We will post any change here and update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
