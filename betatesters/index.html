Beta Testers

Thanks for testing LMT Assistant.

Your feedback shapes what ships. Below is a short walkthrough of what to try, plus a form to send any bugs, questions, or feature ideas straight to me.

i
New here? Finish onboarding first, then open the in-app tutorial. When you sign up, the app walks you through a quick onboarding wizard (business name, license, services, etc.). Once that is done, tap More → Settings → Tutorial for a guided walkthrough of every screen. The tutorial is the fastest way to get oriented before you dig into testing.
$
You will not be charged for the subscription. Please test it. When the app asks you to subscribe to LMT Assistant Pro to access premium features, go ahead and complete the purchase. Because this is a TestFlight build, Apple runs everything against its sandbox, no real money moves and no card is charged. If you see a "[Sandbox]" tag on the purchase sheet, that confirms it. Subscribe, cancel, resubscribe, try the trial, try the annual plan, all of it is fair game and helpful to test.

Two ways to send feedback

Pick whichever is easier in the moment.

1. Inside TestFlight (best for bugs with screenshots). Take a screenshot of the bug. TestFlight pops up a "Send Beta Feedback" sheet automatically. Type a short note, tap Send. The screenshot + your comment go straight to me.
2. The form on this page (best for general thoughts, ideas, or anything that's not a single screen). Fill it out below. It lands in my inbox the same way.

What to test first

A 10-minute walkthrough that hits the most important paths.

  • Sign up + onboarding Create your account, walk through the wizard, set your business name + license + at least one service. Skip anything you don't use; I want to know if any step is confusing.
  • Add a client + create an appointment Try both a studio appointment and an outcall (mobile) appointment with an address. Open the calendar, scroll a few months, tap a day.
  • Run a session timer Start a session from the timer tab. Try landscape mode. Try Spotify if you use it (open a saved playlist). Let it run for a few minutes, then end.
  • Write a SOAP note After ending a session, write a SOAP note. Try the body-diagram, the area-treated picker, and the pressure level. Save it, reopen the client, confirm the note is there.
  • Send an invoice Make an invoice for a client. Add a line item or two. Preview it as a PDF, then either share it or send it via your phone's Mail / Messages.
  • Track an expense + a mileage trip Snap a receipt photo (the AI scanner should pull merchant + total). Log a trip from address A to address B; verify the mileage looks right.
  • Public booking page From the Appts tab, tap the globe icon in the top right to open Online Booking. Enable booking, pick a slug, hit Preview. Try booking yourself a fake appointment from the public link in Safari. Confirm the booking shows up in your appointments list inside the app.
  • Settings + Privacy Toggle dark/light theme. Try Face ID lock if you want it. Glance at "Privacy & Security", none of it should look broken.
