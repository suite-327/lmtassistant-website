2. Description of Service

LMT Assistant is a practice management application designed for licensed massage therapists. The App provides tools for:

  • Client management, scheduling, and recurring appointments
  • Session timing and SOAP note documentation
  • Invoicing with PDF preview and email delivery
  • Expense tracking with AI-powered receipt scanning
  • Income and 1099 tracking with AI document scanning
  • Mileage logging with Google Maps distance calculation and AI booking screenshot scanning
  • Credentials vault for licenses, insurance, CE certifications, and transcripts with AI document scanning
  • State continuing education requirements for all 50 US states
  • Professional goals tracking
  • Booking link generation
  • Therapist network for professional contacts and referrals
  • Encrypted data backup and restore
  • Push notifications for credential renewal reminders
  • Optional integrations (Spotify for session music)
