Billing through Apple

Subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple, so a few things can only be done in your Apple account:

  • Cancel or change your plan: on your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Or visit apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.
  • Request a refund: Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

When you email us

These details help us fix things the first time:

  • Your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version.
  • What you were doing and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, if you can take one.

Please don't send client details. Leave out client names, health information and SOAP notes. We can help without them.

Report a security concern

If you think you have found a security problem, email support@lmtassistant.com with "Security" in the subject. We look at these first.
