Initial Setup

Create Your Account

  1. Download LMT Assistant from the App Store or Google Play
  2. Tap "Sign Up" and enter your email address
  3. Create a strong password (at least 8 characters with numbers and symbols)
  4. Verify your email by clicking the link we send you

Set Up Your Profile

  1. Go to Settings > Account Profile
  2. Add your business name and contact information
  3. Set your default service rates
  4. Add your payment handles (Venmo, Zelle, etc.) for invoices
Pro Tip

Add your frequently used locations (office, regular clients' homes) to Saved Locations for faster mileage logging.

2

Managing Clients

Add Your First Client

  1. Tap the "Clients" tab at the bottom of the screen
  2. Tap the "+" button to add a new client
  3. Enter their name, contact information, and any intake notes
  4. Tap "Save" to create the client profile

What to Include

  • Contact Info: Phone, email, and address for mobile visits
  • Intake Notes: Health history, contraindications, preferences
  • Tags: Categorize clients (e.g., "Monthly Regular", "Sports")
Privacy Note

All client information is encrypted for HIPAA compliance. Only you can access your client data.

Search & Filter

Use the search bar to quickly find clients by name. You can also filter by tags or sort alphabetically or by most recent appointment.

Creating SOAP Notes

What is a SOAP Note?

SOAP notes are a standard format for documenting client sessions:

  • S - Subjective: What the client tells you (complaints, goals)
  • O - Objective: What you observe and find during assessment
  • A - Assessment: Your professional analysis
  • P - Plan: Treatment plan, recommendations, follow-up

Create a SOAP Note

  1. Open a client's profile
  2. Tap "Add SOAP Note"
  3. Select the associated appointment (if any)
  4. Fill in each section
  5. Add any body diagram markings if needed
  6. Save the note
Pro Tip

You can create SOAP note templates for common treatments to save time on documentation.

Invoicing & Payments

Create an Invoice

  1. Go to the Income Vault tab
  2. Tap "New Invoice"
  3. Select a client
  4. Add line items (services, products)
  5. Set tax rate if applicable
  6. Add any notes or terms
  7. Preview and send to client

Payment Methods

Add your payment handles in Settings so they appear on invoices:

  • Venmo
  • Zelle
  • Cash App
  • PayPal

Track Payments

Mark invoices as paid when you receive payment. You can track outstanding invoices and send reminders for overdue payments.

Tracking Expenses

Log an Expense

  1. Go to Expenses tab
  2. Tap "+" to add a new expense
  3. Enter amount, merchant, and date
  4. Select a category (supplies, equipment, marketing, etc.)
  5. Snap a photo of the receipt
  6. Save the expense

Receipt Scanning

Use the camera to scan receipts. LMT Assistant will automatically extract:

  • Merchant name
  • Total amount
  • Date of purchase

Mileage Tracking

  1. Go to the Mileage tab
  2. Tap "+" to log a trip
  3. Enter start and end locations
  4. The app calculates distance automatically
  5. Add notes (which client, purpose)
Tax Tip

The IRS standard mileage rate for 2026 is automatically applied to your mileage logs for accurate deduction tracking.

7

Reports & Tax Prep

Available Reports

  • Income Summary: Total revenue by month, quarter, or year
  • Expense Summary: Expenses categorized for Schedule C
  • Mileage Log: Complete trip history with deduction totals
  • Client Reports: Session history per client

Export for Tax Season

  1. Go to Dashboard > Reports
  2. Select the date range (typically Jan 1 - Dec 31)
  3. Choose which reports to generate
  4. Export as PDF or CSV
  5. Share with your accountant
Pro Tip

Run quarterly reports to estimate your tax obligations and avoid surprises at year end.
