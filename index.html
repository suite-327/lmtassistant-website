A note on scope

A tool you use.

LMT Assistant is not a marketplace. We don't send clients your way or list you in a directory. Your booking page is a link you share with the people who already know you.

Clients find you through your own channels: word of mouth, social, referrals, your website. The app's job is to make the work around those clients easier once they're yours.

Payments stay yours.

We don't process payments. When a client pays you by Venmo, Cash App, Zelle, Stripe, Apple Pay, cash, or anything else, the money moves directly between you and them.

The app records what was paid and how, so your books stay clean. It never touches the transaction, holds funds, or takes a cut.
