Getting Started
LMT Assistant is a comprehensive practice management app designed specifically for Licensed Massage Therapists. It helps you manage clients, schedule appointments, create invoices, track expenses, log mileage, and maintain SOAP notes - all in one secure, HIPAA-compliant platform.
Yes! LMT Assistant is available on both iOS (iPhone and iPad) and Android devices. You can download it from the App Store or Google Play Store.
LMT Assistant offers a free tier with essential features. Premium features are available through our subscription plans. Check our pricing page for current rates and what's included in each plan.
Privacy & Security
Absolutely. We take security seriously:
  • All sensitive client information (PHI) is encrypted using AES-256 encryption
  • Data is encrypted both in transit and at rest
  • We use Supabase with row-level security policies
  • Your encryption keys are unique to your account
  • We never share or sell your data
LMT Assistant is designed with HIPAA compliance in mind. We implement technical safeguards including encryption, access controls, and audit logging. However, HIPAA compliance is a shared responsibility - you should also follow best practices like using strong passwords and keeping your device secure.
Yes. You have full control over your data. You can export your data at any time from the Settings menu. If you choose to delete your account, all your data will be permanently removed from our servers.
Features
From a client's profile, tap "Add SOAP Note" to create a new session note. You can document Subjective observations, Objective findings, Assessment, and Plan. SOAP notes are automatically linked to the client and appointment for easy reference.
Yes! The mileage tracker lets you log trips for outcall appointments. You can enter addresses manually or use GPS to calculate distances. All mileage is categorized and summarized for easy tax reporting at year end.
Create professional invoices by selecting a client, adding services/line items, and setting payment terms. You can preview invoices as PDFs, share them via email or text, and track payment status. Add your payment handles (Venmo, Zelle, etc.) to display on invoices.
Yes! Use your camera to scan receipts and LMT Assistant will automatically extract the merchant, amount, and date using OCR technology. This makes expense tracking quick and paperless.
Account & Support
On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password" and enter your email address. You'll receive a link to reset your password. For security reasons, the link expires after 24 hours.
You can reach our support team at admin@suite-327.com. We typically respond within 24-48 hours. You can also use the "Contact Support" option in the app's Settings menu.
We love hearing from our users! Use the "Request a Feature" option in Settings, or email us directly. Many of our best features came from user suggestions.
